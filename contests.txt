Do not sacrifice your whole week for a contest.

Contests check what you already know. If you spend the whole week
doing contests preparation, you will miss the basics taught in that
week's class. Pay attention in classes & labs, write notes by hand, and
solve assignments. Revise these notes before the contest and this is
how you actually improve your rank without falling behind in studies.


Cons (bad effects)
    1. You miss important classes and lab work. It becomes very hard to catch up later.
    2. You make no notes, so before exams you have nothing to revise.
    3. Too much contest pressure can cause burnout, stress, and sleep loss.
    4. You focus only on one skill and ignore communication, teamwork, and projects.
